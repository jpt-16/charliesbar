Before you come

Good to know

We're cash only

No cards, no checks — it's been that way a long time. There's an ATM in the front bar if you get caught short.

Soda refills aren't free

Worth knowing before the bill lands. Ask for tap water any time and it's on us.

Parking is free, and tight

The lot is ours and it doesn't cost a thing, but it fills up and the spaces are close. Weekend nights, give yourself a minute.

Everyone's welcome

Kid‑friendly, wheelchair accessible, and there's usually a stool open at the bar.

Cheers.

Call ahead and the wings are ready before you finish the drive down Shore Road. Or just take a stool — there's usually one open.
