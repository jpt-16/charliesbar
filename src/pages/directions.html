Getting here & parking

Parking

We have our own lot and it's free. It's also tight, and it fills up on weekend nights — give yourself a minute getting in and out.

Getting here

A few minutes from the Garden State Parkway exit 30, and a short drive over the bridge from Ocean City.

Accessibility

The entrance and dining room are wheelchair accessible. If there's anything we can do to make your visit easier, call ahead and ask.

Cash only

No cards, no checks. There's an ATM in the front bar if you arrive without any.
